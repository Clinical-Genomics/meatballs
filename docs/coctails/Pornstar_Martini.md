# Pornstar Martini 

![bild](../img/star-martini.jpg)

## Ingredients 
- 45 ml vanilla vodka
- 15 ml Passoã (passion fruit liqueur)
- 30 ml passion fruit purée
- 15 ml fresh lime juice
- 15 ml vanilla simple syrup (or vanilla sugar)
- 60 ml chilled Champagne, Prosecco, or sparkling wine (for the sidecar)
- 1/2 fresh passion fruit (for garnish)

## How to make it
1. Shake: Add the vanilla vodka, Passoã, passion fruit purée, lime juice, and vanilla syrup to a cocktail shaker filled with ice. Shake hard for about 10–15 seconds until very cold.
2. Strain: Double-strain the mixture through a fine mesh strainer into a chilled coupe or martini glass.
3. Garnish: Float half a fresh passion fruit on top of the foam.
4. Serve: Pour the chilled sparkling wine into a separate shot glass.
